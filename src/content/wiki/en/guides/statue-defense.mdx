## Why can a busy team still lose control of the statue?

Protecting the statue is the stated objective. Fighting an enemy does not remove the need to watch arrivals. When everyone follows the same threat, the team can stop checking the routes leading to the objective.

Recent [Maniac gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzjdxqnRNXU) illustrates the attention problem: players call out side entrances at **2:08** and discuss the base getting attacked at **5:34–5:38**. Those observations apply to that filmed run; they do not establish a universal map layout.

## What is a useful defense routine?

1. **Look at the approach.** Before engaging, locate the statue and the routes arrivals use in this map.
2. **Inspect before committing.** Use the [disguise checklist](/guides/spot-fakes/) to compare matching arrivals.
3. **Keep a return path.** Choose a position from which you can respond at the statue without getting absorbed in a distant chase.
4. **Leave someone observing.** With a group, agree who will keep watching arrivals while others handle an active fight.
5. **Recheck after each engagement.** Look back at the objective before selecting another target.

This is a recommended team habit. It is not a claim that the game has a formal assigned-defense role or that one class is required to perform it.

## How do you divide attention with teammates?

Use short, specific information. Describe the route or the threat you can actually see, and distinguish a suspicion from a confirmed fight. For example, “watch this approach while I return to the statue” describes an immediate task; “everything unusual is fake” does not.

Do not build the plan around an unverified Ping key or quick-chat shortcut. If you want a particular control, check the current in-game UI. Our launch research found demand for those controls, but the exact current bindings have not been confirmed.

## What should you change after a failed attempt?

| Problem you observed | One change to test |
|---|---|
| Everyone followed the same enemy | Agree on who keeps watching arrivals |
| You could not return in time | Use a closer position with a clearer return path |
| A side route surprised you | Inspect that route before the next wave |
| You confused an innocent with a threat | Compare matching arrivals and note the uncertain feature |
| Equipment distracted you from the objective | Practice the tool separately and reread its description |

Change one habit at a time so you can tell what helped. The [class-selection guide](/guides/classes/) addresses equipment tradeoffs; the [Maniac guide](/guides/maniac-mode/) explains how to prepare for harder footage without copying unverified numbers.
