## What are you trying to do?

Protect the statue while a crowd approaches. Some arrivals are innocent Glaggles; others are Enphosians wearing imperfect disguises. The developer describes enemies that fight back, new arrivals across waves, and bosses. Your task combines reading the crowd with combat and attention to the objective.

This is the **SMILE MORE** version of the game. Old articles can describe earlier equipment or difficulty rules. Start with the current menus rather than copying a dated loadout.

## How do you get into your first run?

1. Open [Enfosi by Tumore on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/78353262320982/Enfosi). Check the creator, especially if you arrived through search.
2. Launch the experience using Roblox. PC is the developer’s recommended device.
3. Find an available lobby and join a run. Recent Maniac footage shows a player creating a lobby at **0:43**, other players joining, and the group starting at **1:13–1:23**. Follow the options actually shown in your current lobby.
4. Locate the statue and watch the approach routes before chasing a target.
5. Read the description of your equipment. The current shop explains what individual tools do; see the [class-selection checklist](/guides/classes/) for what to compare.

This guide does not promise a particular button layout, lobby capacity, or class unlock. Those details were not independently tested on a new account.

## What should you do when the crowd arrives?

Learn what a normal arrival looks like before deciding another is an impostor. Compare the same type of Glaggle, and use more than one visible feature when possible. The [disguise inspection guide](/guides/spot-fakes/) explains both the community checklist and why a single unusual feature can mislead you.

When combat begins, keep checking the statue. A fight away from the objective can occupy the whole team while another threat moves through. The [statue-defense routine](/guides/statue-defense/) turns that problem into a simple team habit.

## How should you judge your first attempt?

Choose one thing to improve after each run:

| What went wrong? | What to try next |
|---|---|
| You attacked an arrival you did not understand | Compare two arrivals of the same type before committing |
| You lost track of the objective | Stay within a route you can use to return to the statue |
| A tool behaved unexpectedly | Read its current shop description and test it deliberately |
| Everyone chased the same fight | Agree who will continue watching the approach |
| You followed an old guide | Check its version against the [SMILE MORE summary](/updates/smile-more/) |

These are practice recommendations based on the objective and observed footage, not a guaranteed winning build. Once the basic loop is familiar, use the [Maniac preparation guide](/guides/maniac-mode/) to decide what to learn next.
