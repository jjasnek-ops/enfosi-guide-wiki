## What does the developer actually confirm?

Enphosians hide among innocent Glaggles using imperfect disguises. The current Roblox description confirms that identification is part of the objective. It does not provide a complete visual checklist or a guaranteed test for every enemy, difficulty, and modifier.

Treat the following as **community inspection guidance**. It is a way to look carefully, not permission to attack every character with an unfamiliar appearance.

## What should you compare?

Compare arrivals **of the same type**. Two different Glaggles can have different normal appearances. The community [Glaggles reference](https://enfosi.fandom.com/wiki/Glaggles) suggests faces, color and torso markings as possible differences.

| Check | What to inspect | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Face | Missing or different facial details compared with a matching arrival | A distant or obstructed view may be incomplete |
| Color | A mismatch against the same type | Lighting and unfamiliar variants can confuse the comparison |
| Torso | A missing, different or misplaced marking | Recent Maniac players report appearances they initially misread |
| Context | Whether you recognize this arrival in the selected mode | Earlier-version examples are not a complete current reference |

The visual examples in older community pages may predate SMILE MORE. We have not verified an exhaustive current disguise roster or an exact rule for each difficulty.

## Why should you avoid a one-detail decision?

In [recent Maniac footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzjdxqnRNXU&t=146s), at **2:26–2:37**, players discuss an arrival with a shirt they thought looked fake. They interpret it as legitimate after an unexpected consequence. This is one group’s report, not a verified formula for what triggers that consequence.

The practical lesson is to compare more carefully. Do not turn the players’ explanation into a universal hidden mechanic. If you cannot identify an arrival, improve your viewing angle and compare it with a matching type while remaining close enough to respond at the statue.

## What do you do after a fight starts?

Keep looking back at the objective. The game’s official description warns that revealed Enphosians fight back; an extended fight can pull attention away from the approach routes. Use the [statue-defense routine](/guides/statue-defense/) to keep someone watching the objective.

If an unfamiliar tool makes the situation harder to read, return to its current description. The [class guide](/guides/classes/) explains how to separate a class choice from a recognition problem. For a first run, follow the [beginner sequence](/guides/beginner-guide/).

## What remains unverified?

- A complete SMILE MORE list of legitimate and fake appearances.
- Whether any one visual tell applies across all difficulties and modifiers.
- Exact penalties or hidden triggers after attacking an innocent.
