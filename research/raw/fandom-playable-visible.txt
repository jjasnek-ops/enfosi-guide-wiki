This page is about Glaggles (Playable). You may be looking for Glaggles.

There are currently 8 playable Glagglers. Each Glaggler will have their own respective starting loadout to their weapon arsenal, being the first two items on their arsenal, and will morph into the Glaggler the player picks. They will also receive buffs and nerfs tied to the Glaggler.

The Sobber is currently the only special class as it can't be purchased directly from the shop, however has no benefits.
